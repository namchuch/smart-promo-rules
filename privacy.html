Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information Smart Promo Rules ("the app") handles when a merchant installs it on a Shopify store.

Information the app accesses

  • Discount settings. The promotion rules a merchant configures: promotion type, amounts, and the selected collections and products. They are stored inside Shopify, as metafields on the discount.
  • Cart contents at checkout. While Shopify calculates discounts, the app's function receives the cart lines (quantities, prices, product and collection membership) in order to compute the discount. This runs inside Shopify's infrastructure.
  • Interface language. The language chosen for the app's forms is stored as a setting on the store.

The app does not request access to customer records, orders, or any personal information.

Information the app stores

The app does not operate a database of its own and does not store personal information about a merchant's customers. Its settings are kept in Shopify metafields that belong to the merchant's store.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Data requests and deletion

Because the app keeps no customer data, there is nothing to export or delete on our side. The app responds to Shopify's mandatory privacy webhooks (customer data request, customer redact, shop redact). Uninstalling the app removes its access to the store.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
