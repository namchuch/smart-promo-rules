Help with Smart Promo Rules.

Getting started

  • In the Shopify admin, open Discounts and click Create discount.
  • Choose Promotion rules, then pick a promotion type and fill in its settings.
  • Choose the collections or products the promotion applies to, give the discount a title, and save.

Common questions

The discount does not appear in the cart. Check that the discount is active, that the products in the cart belong to the selected collections, and that "Exclude products that are already on sale" is not filtering them out. Automatic discounts from this app do not combine with other discounts unless you allow it under Combinations.

The form shows "Update permissions". Click it once. This appears after an app update that changes the permissions the app needs.

Can I change the form language? Yes. Use the Language field at the top of the form. The choice is remembered for the store.
