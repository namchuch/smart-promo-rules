Smart Promo Rules

Promotion rules for Shopify discounts.

Smart Promo Rules adds promotion types to the Discounts page of the Shopify admin:

  • A fixed price for any quantity (2 for X, 3 for X, 5 for X)
  • Buy X get Y, with the cheapest items free or at a percentage off
  • Bundles: an item from one group with an item from another, at a set price or a percentage off
  • Cart tiers: a higher percentage for a bigger cart
  • A gift above a cart amount
  • An extra percentage off sale items only, or sitewide with exclusions

Each discount is spread across the items of the set in proportion to their price, so a partial return refunds exactly what the customer paid.

The app has no separate dashboard. After installing, go to Discounts → Create discount → Promotion rules.
